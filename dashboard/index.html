Indicator Backtest

Reference

Overview

Reference for all controls, strategies, and result columns in this backtest tool.

Tests rule-based technical strategies on raw OHLCV data: entry/exit signals are generated purely from indicator logic (MA cross, Bollinger Bands, RSI, etc.), no external inputs.

Use the sidebar to navigate to any specific control or strategy. The P&L formula and caching reference are at the bottom.

MA Cross

Signals when a fast moving average crosses a slow moving average.

Long signalFast MA crosses above slow MA (golden cross).
Short signalFast MA crosses below slow MA (death cross).
Fast / Slow periodLookback candles for each MA. Fast default 20, Slow default 50.
MA typeEMA (more weight on recent candles) or SMA (equal weight).

Best for trending markets with clear momentum shifts. Produces false signals in choppy, sideways conditions.

Price vs MA

Signals when the closing price crosses above or below a single moving average.

Long signalClose crosses above the MA.
Short signalClose crosses below the MA.
PeriodMA lookback window (default 50).
MA typeEMA or SMA.

Simpler and faster-reacting than MA Cross. More signals, more noise. Works well as a trend filter on higher timeframes.

Bollinger Bands

Bands at ±N standard deviations from a rolling SMA. Signals on band bounces (mean-reversion).

Long signalPrevious close below lower band AND current close back above it (bounce).
Short signalPrevious close above upper band AND current close back below it (rejection).
PeriodRolling window for the mid-band SMA (default 20).
Std DevBand width multiplier. 2.0 covers ~95% of price action (default 2.0).

Mean-reversion strategy. Works in ranging markets; loses in strong trends where price walks the band.

BB Channel Rider

A state-machine strategy that rides the full width of the Bollinger channel, flipping between long and short as price bounces between bands.

Entry

Long entryLow wick touches or crosses below the lower band.
Short entryHigh wick touches or crosses above the upper band.
Entry priceThe band value at the time of entry (lower band for longs, upper band for shorts).

Take Profit (tracking)

Long TPHigh wick touches or crosses the current upper band value (tracked each candle).
Short TPLow wick touches or crosses the current lower band value (tracked each candle).
Flip on TPTP hit → immediately opens opposite position (long→short or short→long) with no cooldown. The flip is skipped if the trend filter blocks the new direction.

Stop Loss & SL Snap

SL %Fixed percentage from entry. Set via the SL % slider in the sidebar.
SL Snap thresholdOnce price moves threshold % of the mid→band distance toward TP, the SL is moved to the mid-band. One-time and monotone (never lowers a long SL, never raises a short SL).

Example — Long at $79,000, mid=$82,000, upper=$85,000, threshold=75%:

snap_trigger = $82,000 + 0.75 × ($85,000 − $82,000) = $84,250

When high ≥ $84,250 → SL moves from $75,050 up to $82,000 (fixed)

Cooldown after SL

Cooldown NAfter a SL hit, N candles must close on the correct side of the entry band before the next entry is allowed.
Long cooldownN candles closing above the lower band. Wicks below do not reset the count.
Short cooldownN candles closing below the upper band.
N = 0No cooldown — re-enter on the very next band touch.

Trend Filter (optional)

Trend EMAConfigurable EMA period (default 200). Long entries only when close > EMA (uptrend context). Short entries only when close < EMA (downtrend context).
On chartYellow EMA line overlaid on the price chart when trend filter is enabled.

When to use this strategy

Best in ranging, sideways markets where price repeatedly bounces between bands. Avoid or enable the trend filter in strong directional trends, as price can walk the band and cause repeated stop-outs on the counter-trend side.

RSI

Relative Strength Index (0–100). Signals when RSI exits oversold or overbought zones. Sub-plot shown below the price chart.

Long signalRSI crosses above the oversold threshold (default 30).
Short signalRSI crosses below the overbought threshold (default 70).
PeriodRSI lookback (default 14). Shorter = more reactive, more noise.
Oversold / OverboughtZone thresholds. Crossing the oversold line upward = long; crossing overbought downward = short.

Counter-trend / mean-reversion. RSI can stay extreme for many candles in strong trends, causing frequent stop-outs.

Indicator Backtest — Chart Controls

ZoomCtrl + scroll wheel to zoom in/out. Both price chart and sub-plot zoom in sync.
PanScroll wheel (no Ctrl) pans left/right. Click and drag also pans. Both charts move together.
Reset ZoomButton top-right of the price chart restores the full view.
B markersGreen circle = trade entry (resolved). Grey circle = open trade (no exit yet).
S markersRed circle = trade exit (TP or SL hit). Hover for tooltip with direction, outcome, and P&L.
Sub-plot syncRSI sub-plot x-axis is locked to the price chart. Zooming or panning one moves both. Vertical grid lines are mirrored exactly.

Indicator Backtest — Trade Table

Entry DateTimestamp of the signal candle — when the trade opens.
Entry / SL / TPPrices used. For BB Channel, TP shows "—" (band-based, not fixed).
Move %Raw price move before leverage. Positive = moved in trade direction.
P&LNet dollar profit/loss after fees: (net_pnl % / 100) × trade_size.
OutcomeTAKE PROFIT, STOP LOSS, or OPEN (still running at end of data).
Exit DateCandle where SL or TP was hit. Blank for open trades.

P&L Formula

price_move = (exit − entry) / entry ← long

price_move = (entry − exit) / entry ← short

gross_pnl % = price_move × leverage × 100

fee % = 2 × fee_rate × leverage × 100 ← entry + exit

net_pnl % = gross_pnl % − fee %

pnl_dollar = (net_pnl % / 100) × trade_size

Fees apply only to resolved trades (TP or SL). Open trades show gross P&L with no fee deduction.
